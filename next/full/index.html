Heavy appliances and permanently connected loads

Enter the nameplate rating of each load. Alberta STANDATA 24-ECB-008 requires the nameplate rating of each load over 1500 W to be used, and it puts a domestic hot water storage tank heater in the other loads group, not in the tankless group.

Electric vehicle supply equipment

Alberta STANDATA 24-ECI-086 requires the maximum adjustable setting in the calculation. A lower setting can count only where the charger has a manual switch inside the equipment and an electrician has to set it. A charger a customer can turn back up to 48 A gets counted at 48 A. EVEMS controls demand, it does not create service capacity.

Apartment building

The unit inputs above describe one typical dwelling unit. Rule 8-202 3) a) applies the diversity ladder to the unit loads with EVSE, space heating and air conditioning taken out first.

Branch circuits

Rule 8-304 sets the maximum number of outlets per circuit and Rule 8-104 2) says the calculated load in a circuit shall not exceed the ampere rating of the circuit. Branch circuit counts do not add to the service calculation on their own, the loads above are what the service is sized on.

Measured demand alternative, Rule 8-106 8)

Where loads are being added to an existing service, the augmented load may be calculated from the measured maximum demand over the most recent 12 months plus the new loads. Run it alongside the full calculation. When the two disagree, the disagreement is the conversation with the customer.
